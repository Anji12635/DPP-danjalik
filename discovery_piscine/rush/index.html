02 / THE STORY BEHIND THE SCREEN

From the page
to the screen.

Every unforgettable film begins with a story. The Culpa Mía universe began with Spanish author Mercedes Ron and her bestselling Culpables book trilogy: Culpa Mía, Culpa Tuya and Culpa Nuestra.

At its heart is Noah, whose life changes when her mother marries a wealthy man. She meets Nick, her new stepbrother, and their clashing personalities give way to a complicated romance. Across three chapters, love is challenged by family secrets, jealousy, ambition, heartbreak and the difficult choices that come with growing up.

THE BOOKS BECOME FILMS

The popularity of Mercedes Ron's novels brought Noah and Nick's story to the screen. Prime Video adapted the books into a Spanish-language film trilogy, bringing the characters and their world to life for audiences around the globe. Director Domingo González helmed the original Spanish films, with Nicole Wallace and Gabriel Guevara starring as Noah and Nick.
