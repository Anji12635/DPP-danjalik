The Storyline

A Love That Breaks Every Rule

Noah's life changes when she moves away from her familiar world to live with her mother and her mother's wealthy new husband. There she meets Nick, her new stepbrother, whose confident personality and rebellious lifestyle seem completely different from her own.

At first, they clash. But as they spend more time together, their rivalry turns into an attraction neither can easily ignore. Nick introduces Noah to a world of underground racing, unexpected friendships and dangerous situations, while Noah discovers that there is much more to him than meets the eye.

As secrets from their past begin to surface, Noah and Nick must confront their feelings and the consequences of choosing a relationship that challenges the rules around them.

A romantic drama filled with tension, secrets, racing and unexpected turns.
